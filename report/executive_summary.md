## Policy & Compliance Notice

A significant compliance concern was identified during the research phase of this project. Using Apple Pay or Google Pay to sell in-game tokens directly violates the policies of both platforms.

Full details including exact policy references, verbatim quotes, and consequence breakdowns are documented in [`policy_compliance.md`](./policy_compliance.md).
